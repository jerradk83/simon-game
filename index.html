HOW TO PLAY SIMON

1
Watch & Listen: Simon lights up a color pad and plays a tone.
2
Repeat the Pattern: Press the exact same sequence of colors in order.
3
Progress: Each round Simon adds one new color to the sequence. The pace quickens as you advance!
4
Modes: In Normal Mode, you have 3 lives to recover from mistakes. In Strict Mode, one misstep ends the game!
